Profile

Professional Summary

I work on large-scale genomic data analysis for therapeutic target discovery and precision medicine, with experience across GWAS, multi-ancestry meta-analysis, fine-mapping, and polygenic risk score modelling for complex cardiometabolic and renal traits. My work combines statistical method development with reproducible analytical pipelines in R, Python, Linux, HPC, and SLURM environments.

Career

Professional Experience

Nov 2022 - Present

Research Associate, MRC Biostatistics Unit, University of Cambridge

Developing environment-adjusted genetic analysis methods for cardiometabolic traits in African populations under the supervision of Assistant Professor Jennifer Asimit.

  • Developed environment-adjusted meta-regression of GWAS across cohort-level summary statistics and environmental factors including BMI, sex, and urban status.
  • Built environment-adjusted single-trait multi-ancestry fine-mapping methods for multiple causal variants and cohort-specific genetic interpretation.
  • Conducted cross-population PGS transferability analyses across diverse African cohorts using fine-mapped SNP sets.
  • Integrated and quality-controlled heterogeneous GWAS summary statistics for downstream meta-analysis.
2022 - Present

Post-GWAS Analysis, African-Ancestry Kidney Function Meta-Analysis

Contributed to ancestry-specific genetic loci discovery for kidney function, led by Dr. Oyesola Ojewunmi at Queen Mary University of London.

  • Performed post-GWAS meta-analyses and fine-mapping of eGFR-associated genomic risk loci across East, West, and South African populations.
  • Prepared reproducible analysis reports and presented findings to collaborators for downstream translational research.
Mar 2022 - Nov 2022

Postdoctoral Fellow, Agricultural Genomics Institute at Shenzhen, CAAS

Developed statistical methods for testing gene-by-gene and gene-by-environment interactions with multiple complex traits, supervised by Professor Gengjie Jia.

  • Developed generalized linear model methods for multiple phenotypes and GxG/GxE interactions.
  • Applied the methods to European cohort datasets for type 2 diabetes and hypertension.
  • Validated and benchmarked methods against current statistical genetics approaches.

Programming & Software

Identifies genetic variants associated with traits across cohorts while adjusting for differing environmental exposures.

Environment-adjusted MR-MEGA fine-mapping across multiple cohorts while allowing multiple causal variants.

GiMat
